SECURITY & FRAUD AWARENESS

Your security comes first.

Clear rules help you recognize suspicious requests and protect your account.

Rocher will never ask you for:
  • Your online banking username or password by email, phone, social media, or unsolicited message.
  • Your card PIN, CVV, complete card details, or one-time security code through an unsolicited request.
  • A payment or transfer simply to unlock, verify, secure, or release your account.

How to spot phishing

Be cautious with unexpected messages, urgent demands, unfamiliar links, requests for credentials, or anyone asking you to move money for a supposed security check. If a message looks suspicious, do not click its links or reply with confidential information.

Before you sign in

Use the official Rocher website and navigate to Client login yourself. Check the address bar before entering credentials. Do not use login links supplied by an unsolicited message.

If someone contacts you

Stop the conversation if you are asked for confidential credentials or payment. Use the official customer-care route on this website to verify the communication.
